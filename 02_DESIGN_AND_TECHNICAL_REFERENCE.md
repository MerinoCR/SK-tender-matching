**Proposed scope record:** Case/run ID, approved group IDs and filters, taxonomy version, known descendant policy, reported totals, returned and unique counts, retrieval interval, exclusions, errors and `complete` status. A partial run must remain explicitly partial.

### Custom-field ID-to-label mapping

- **Historical assistant analysis:** `external_field_data` reportedly contained ID/name/value metadata in all 60 sample records, with matching top-level and nested custom-field values in that sample. This is not a verified universal schema.
- **Proposed method:** Build a versioned dictionary of custom-field ID, original label, language and observed location from a reviewed sample or approved field metadata. The three IDs above are historical mappings to verify, not permanent contracts.
- **Proposed safeguards:** Keep original IDs and labels, log renamed/unknown fields, compare duplicate representations and preserve disagreements. Do not silently equate differently labeled fields.
- **Open question:** `fieldList`, `fullFieldList` and `productSettings` were also mentioned as Network request names. Their endpoints and suitability for an authoritative dictionary have not been validated.

## Data quality and interpretation risks

- **Historical assistant analysis, empty fields:** English descriptions were reported populated in 12/60 records, Chinese descriptions in 21/60 and Chinese configuration in 59/60; Key Spec, SKU attributes and product size were reported empty throughout. These are sample-only findings, not catalog statistics.
- **Agreed requirement, multilingual evidence:** Keep source languages separate; translated interpretations must not replace originals. Populated text may still be incomplete or contain order/customization notes rather than specifications.
- **Historical assistant analysis, configurations:** Repeated models reportedly had different configurations. Evaluate the exact SKU; do not merge plugs, accessories, connectivity or other features across variants.
- **Historical assistant analysis, contradictions:** A sample SKU reportedly had conflicting standards in separate Chinese fields. Preserve both claims and seek clarification rather than choosing the favorable field.
- **Historical assistant analysis, classification:** Uncategorized/misclassified products and equipment components were reported. No reliable component separator or active/inactive flag interpretation has been established.
- **Open question, evidence completeness:** List responses reportedly contained useful descriptions, but completeness versus detail views and attachments remains untested. Additional detail retrieval requires a demonstrated need and separate authorization.

## Authentication and confidentiality safeguards

- **Agreed requirement:** Authentication stays on the user's PC. Never request passwords, MFA codes, cookies, tokens, authentication headers, full unredacted cURL commands or HAR files in chat.
- **Agreed requirement:** Do not store secrets or signed access links in Project files. Remove entire signed URLs, not just one query parameter.
- **Proposed safeguards:** Use a strict technical-field allowlist and review free text before handoff. Exclude employee/customer information, internal commercial values, permissions and unrelated metadata; selecting technical fields alone does not guarantee confidentiality.
- **Historical implementation claim, not inspected:** Earlier scripts were described as reading a local clipboard request without saving authentication. This draft neither verifies that behavior nor recommends reuse without source review.
- **Open question:** Confirm company approval, permitted retrieval method, retention/access rules and safe diagnostic logging before development. Use only authorized, read-only access.
